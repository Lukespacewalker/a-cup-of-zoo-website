## Shared prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create ONE small premium watercolor vignette for a Zoo Clubs membership tier on the A Cup of Zoo animal cafe website. Traditional hand-painted naturalistic watercolor, fine brown pencil details, soft feathered brush edges, restrained sepia brown, cream, muted olive and honey ochre palette. Match an elegant warm Japanese garden cafe with textured paper and lifelike friendly animals, not flat vector, not cartoon emoji, not 3D. Center the complete compact composition in a square with generous transparent negative space, entirely contained including tails and ears. Transparent alpha background; only a tiny subtle watercolor ground wash under the subjects. The artwork will display at about 130px wide, so keep silhouettes clear and objects simple. NO text, numbers, badges, logos, lettering, frames or watermark.

## Tier subjects

- **friend.png / Friend:** Subject: one small fluffy cream-and-tan rabbit sitting beside a little olive green sprout, ears upright, a gentle welcoming expression. The simplest welcoming first tier; calm and airy.
- **buddy.png / Buddy:** Subject: two golden brown meerkats sitting close side by side, one upright and one slightly leaning toward the other, with a few tiny muted olive leaves at their feet. Depict a warm friendship; compact, clear silhouette.
- **besties.png / Besties:** Subject: two friendly cats, a ginger tabby and a cream cat, nestled beside a small woven basket of leafy animal feed with one carrot visible. Natural feline anatomy, soft content expressions; subtle sage-green ribbon tied on the basket. Warm intimate friends.
- **soulmate.png / Soulmate:** Subject: a pair of red foxes curled up affectionately together with their fluffy tails wrapped close; two tiny simple ceramic cafe cups placed at their feet, one cream and one muted terracotta. Natural animal anatomy, soft brushwork, intimate calm cafe mood.
- **family.png / Family:** Subject: a small loving group of three animals nestled together: one ginger tabby cat, one cream rabbit with upright ears, and one golden brown meerkat. A tiny kraft gift box with a honey ochre ribbon at their feet suggests a special keepsake. Cozy close family, balanced and compact. No additional animals.
